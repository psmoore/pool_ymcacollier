The evening that isn't there

The Hours page lists North Campus lap swim until 7:30pm Monday through Thursday. On Tuesday and Thursday, the pool schedule has a high school swim meet from 3 to 9pm, and on Monday, Wednesday and Friday high school practice from 3 to 6pm.

Here the evening gap is on the calendar, so a swimmer sees it before driving over, and South Campus's two open lanes show up as the place to go instead.
