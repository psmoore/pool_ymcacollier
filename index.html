Where is the pool schedule?

“Can I swim laps at North Campus at 5pm on Tuesday?”

The Hours page says yes: lap swim until 7:30pm. The Open Swim page says to “check pool schedules” and links to a class schedule that has no pool times in it. The pool times are on the Schedules page, under the heading “Group Fitness Schedule”, one day at a time, once you tick Pool — and there the answer is no: the pool is reserved for a high school swim meet from 3 to 9pm.

On this calendar the answer is the same everywhere, because every page reads the same schedule. When the high school season ends, the staff change it once in an AI chat, and lap swim is back on the Hours, Swimming and Schedules pages that evening.

Questions for the aquatics team

Everything on these calendars comes from what the Y publishes. These are the places where two of its own pages disagree, where a schedule isn't published, or where we had to make a call.

  • ConflictNorth Campus: lap swim until 7:30pm?
    The Hours page gives North Campus lap swim Monday–Thursday 5:30am–7:30pm. The schedule closes the pool 3–6pm Monday, Wednesday and Friday for high school swim & dive practice, and 3–9pm Tuesday and Thursday for high school meets. The calendar follows the schedule.
  • ConflictSouth Campus: two lanes, or the whole pool?
    The Hours page says South Campus lap swim is 7am–3:30pm, then 4:30–6pm on “2 lanes only”, with swim team 3:30–6pm. The schedule lists Open Lap Swim 7am–6pm with no break. The calendar follows the Hours page, because it names the lanes.
  • GapWhen does the high school season end?
    The practices and the Tuesday and Thursday meets are listed as repeating every week with no end date. Say when the season ends, and lap swim comes back on the calendar that evening.
  • GapNorth Campus swim lessons
    The swim lessons page says group lessons run at North Campus and South Campus. Registration lists fall sessions only at Marco Island.
  • GapTeam and clinic times
    The Competitive Swim Team, stroke clinics, synchronized swimming and Splash Ball have pages but no published times. The Youth Swim Team's weekday 3:30–6pm comes from the Hours page.
  • OursLap lanes, program area and team lanes
    Neither campus says which lanes lap swim keeps while classes, lessons and teams are in the water. We split each pool into a lap lane area and a program area, plus team lanes at South Campus, so the calendar can show them side by side.
